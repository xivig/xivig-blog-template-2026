Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2026 · Version 2.4

1. Information We Collect

We respect your privacy and process personal information strictly in accordance with global data privacy frameworks including the GDPR and CCPA.

2. Local Theme Preferences

Theme preference state (`xivig-theme`) is stored locally in your browser via `localStorage` solely to preserve dark/light appearance without third-party tracking beacons.

3. Analytics & Newsletter Subscriptions

Email addresses voluntarily provided to newsletter forms are used exclusively to deliver requested editorial updates. Zero personal profiles are shared with advertising brokers.

4. Your Privacy Rights

You retain full rights to access, export, or request deletion of any stored subscriber record by contacting hello@xivig.com.
