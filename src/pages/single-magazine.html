Public-key ceremonies move authentication guarantees out of fragile human memory and directly into origin-bound silicon.

Phishing-resistant authentication starts with the cryptographic origin check enforced natively by browsers and mobile OS keychains. Unlike SMS OTPs or static TOTP secrets that an attacker proxy can relay in real time, WebAuthn signatures bind every challenge cryptographically to the verified relying party domain.
